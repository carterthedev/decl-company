About Diamond Engineering

Premium interior solutions designed with elegance, durability, and precision craftsmanship.

Founded in August 2017, Diamond Engineering has grown into a trusted supplier of interior decoration materials. With a main warehouse in Ndeiya Mboroti, Kiambu County, and an outlet in Kikuyu town, we serve small and medium hardware businesses across the region.
